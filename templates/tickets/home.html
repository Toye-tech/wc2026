Africa's Record Turnout

FOR AFRICA
BY AFRICANS

The 2026 FIFA World Cup delivered the largest African representation in tournament history — ten nations reached the finals for the first time ever, after CAF's final African slot went to DR Congo following a play-off decision against Nigeria. Morocco and South Africa went furthest, with Morocco reaching the quarterfinals and South Africa qualifying for a first-ever knockout round.

This portal remains open for anyone who purchased a ticket through us — bookings and e-tickets can still be looked up anytime. All past transactions were processed in USD via Paystack, trusted across African banks.

FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Is ticket booking still open?

No — with the tournament complete, ticket sales and new bookings through this site are closed. If you already purchased a ticket, you can still look up your booking and e-ticket at any time.

How do I retrieve a ticket I already bought?

Use the "My Bookings" page and search by the email address or reference code you used at checkout. Your booking details and e-ticket will still be accessible there.

Where can I relive the tournament?

Head to the Tournament Archive for the final recap, every knockout result, Africa's campaign, the individual awards, and match highlight videos.

Was my past payment information safe?

Yes. All payments were processed by Paystack, which is PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant — the highest standard of payment security. We never stored your card details directly.

When's the next World Cup?

2030 — jointly hosted by Spain, Portugal and Morocco, with symbolic centenary matches also played in Uruguay, Argentina and Paraguay. Spain arrive as defending champions.
